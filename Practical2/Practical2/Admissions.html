Admission Information

Welcome to the Admissions page. Students can find information about the admission process, eligibility, important dates, and required documents here.

Admission Process

  1. Fill out the online admission form.
  2. Upload the required documents.
  3. Submit the application.
  4. Wait for document verification.
  5. Pay the admission fees after confirmation.

Required Documents

  • 10th Marksheet
  • 12th/Diploma Marksheet
  • School Leaving Certificate
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Passport Size Photograph
